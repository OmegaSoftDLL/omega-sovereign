**Omega Sovereign (`v5.0`, Edition 2026)** is the institutional full-stack native flagship application and binary-assurance showcase of **[Lume `v1.1.0`](https://github.com/OmegaSoftDLL/Lume)**, maintained by **Ricardo Bueno (`@OmegaSoftDLL`)** and **Omega DeepTech Systems Inc. (ODSI) / LumeLabs**.

## Governance & Contribution Standards

1. **100% Native Lume Purity:** All core engines (`.src`), surface domain modules (`modules/*.lume`), formal certificates (`.cert`), and native desktop/kernel synthesizers are authored exclusively in Lume `v1.1.0` with pure LF (`\n`) line endings.
2. **Mandatory Formal & Binary Assurance:** Every change to computational or security-critical logic must pass `lume prove`, `lume certify`, `lume verify-cert --check-source`, `lume tv --exec`, `lume smt-check`, `lume mcdc --exec` (`0` uncovered conditions), and `lume safety-envelope`.
3. **Bilingual Institutional Documentation (`EN` & `PT-BR`):** Architecture manuals, wiki references, and desktop studio inspection cards support both international English and Brazilian Portuguese engineering teams.
